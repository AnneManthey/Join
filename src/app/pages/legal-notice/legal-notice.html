About this Project

Join is a Kanban-style task management app built with Angular and Supabase for organizing work and tracking progress. The Issue Collector extends Join with an AI-powered intake (n8n & Google Gemini) that automatically transforms stakeholder requests via form or email into "Triage" tickets and provides automated status updates.

The original design is owned by Developer Akademie GmbH.
